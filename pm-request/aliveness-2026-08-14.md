# Making the room feel alive — research findings

**Date:** 2026-08-14 · **Status:** research complete, nothing built, nothing green-lit.
**Ask:** "I want the agent to feel alive — like the app is alive."

**Method.** Seven independent investigations — latency, ambient presence, motion, voice,
embodiment, initiative, and the outside literature — each one adversarially verified by a second
pass whose job was to refute it: open every cited line, kill any mechanism that cannot work under
this codebase's constraints, and kill any idea that is technically possible but fights the
product's posture. Every finding below survived that. Every proposal carries the verse it answers
to, its decided failure behaviour if it opens an I/O path, and its smallest shippable version.

Four of the tier-1 claims were additionally spot-checked by hand before this document was written
(`AiPane.tsx:789`, `ollama_lifecycle.rs:26`, `auto_index.rs:59-63`, the absent
`.composer-card.busy` rule, `driver.ts:171`). All four confirmed.

Related: `pm-request/personalization-session-2026-08-14.md` — the spoken/drawn session design,
which this research repeatedly points back to as the right home for spoken-presence work.

---

## The thesis

Arcelle is not under-animated; it is **under-honest in nine specific places and mis-aimed in four more**. Across seven independent investigations, almost every finding that survived adversarial review has the same shape: *the room already knows something, and either says nothing, says it somewhere nobody is looking, or says something that stopped being true.* A queued job slides a progress bar over work that has not started. The app SIGTERMs a model it warmed for thirty minutes because a second constant says five. Home tells you to start a scan that is already running. The agent is told "file viewer open: Workflows" — a file that does not exist. Auto-describe, switched off, still describes. Meanwhile almost every *refuted* proposal was an attempt to add a **new** signal on top of one that was merely lying — a nib during the thinking state, a fourth "Thinking" chip, an ambient drone, a spoken filler. So: **aliveness here is correspondence, not motion.** Every visible sign must map to work actually happening, and every fact the room already holds must reach a surface the user is actually looking at — which, given that the AI pane ships hidden in two layout presets (`useLayout.ts:112,124`) and Activity is behind a click (`state.ts:455`), means exactly two surfaces: **the status bar, and Home.** Ration them, tell the truth on them, and the creature appears without a single new subsystem.

## Already fine — do not touch

A rigorous synthesis has to say where there is no work, and here there is a great deal of it.

**The job/approval half of presence needs nothing.** One definition of busy (`activity.ts:33-42`) consumed by three surfaces with the rationale written down; the active/parked/history split biased safely; history bounded at 25 with the truncation stated; the Activity tab dot distinguishing "needs you" from "is running" by *shape*, with the words in its aria-label (`AiPane.tsx:99-113`); the scheduler generation-pinned with at-most-one catch-up (`scheduler.rs:92-168`); an auto-started job forbidden from carrying a `fileId` (`jobs.rs:795-802`). Crash recovery is already continuity of action: a `queued` job stays queued and `pump_on_open` resumes it (`jobs.rs:339-350`, `queue.rs:157-163`), parked under `PARKED_BY_EXIT` rather than a Stop the user never pressed.

**The agent's hands are complete.** Numbered marks, no fabricated selectors ever, WeakRef re-checked at act time, a Set-of-Marks badge layer, and a ghost ring whose reduced-motion fallback stays *visible* because "it is the only report the user gets that the agent is about to act" (`driver.css:70-79`). The consent door is enforced twice in code — at the walker and again at act time — and there is no `set_setting` tool. Agent-composed workflows save as DRAFTS (`workflow.rs:3478`).

**The turn's structure is right.** `asking` flips synchronously before any await, so Nielsen's 0.1 s band is met with no spinner. The vision pass was already moved to *after* the answer. `num_ctx` is bucketed so the warm preload and the first real call agree. Tool waits are named, and `test_every_registry_tool_has_a_label` fails the build if one is not. Streaming TTS already covers model latency with ~2× headroom, and `effectiveDuration` correctly compensates the detuned archetype copies.

**The motion language is close to exemplary.** Three duration tokens and one easing; reduced-motion coverage effectively complete; keyframes written so `animation: none` lands on the resting value; a seeded wobble the Rust round-trip tests depend on. And the house has already *tried and reverted* ambient motion twice, in writing (`agentGraph.css:198-203`; `composer.css:788-791`).

**Grounding and attribution are solved.** `open_file`/`annotate_file` ground the model's quote against the file's real text, hard-error when nothing is close, and flag `approx` — and the annotation chip already renders Verified vs approximate (`ChatPane.tsx:550, 630-676`), with a comment that is the best statement of this product's posture anywhere in the tree.

Do not restructure any of the above. Several refuted proposals died precisely because they would have.

## Tier 1 — do first: nearly free, changes the feel immediately

**Moves 1–16.** Every one is a defect in correspondence, every one is tiny, and none opens a new I/O path except #15 (which carries its swallow-with-a-reason). Together they cost perhaps a day and they remove: one fake progress animation, one self-inflicted cold start, two silent failure paths, one settings lie, one fabricated fact fed to the agent, one focus theft, one corrupt filename, one premature claim, one mislabelled wait, one redundant IPC call, and one stale badge layer.

> *"Errors should never pass silently. Unless explicitly silenced… Silence only as a visible decision with a written reason; a bare empty catch is a shrug."* — and *"Names should tell the truth. A name that needs a comment is unfinished work."*

Ship these before anything is built. They are also the tier most likely to be skipped, because none of them looks like a feature.

## Tier 2 — a real build

**Moves 17–26.** These add a channel or reshape a query: the room learning what you are looking at, memory selection told the truth about its own units, chats ordered by conversation rather than by birth, the file open acknowledged, the filler's work announced, the chunker and the offline coupling covered by tests, the sketch reveal made honest, hands-free brought to the field's window, the first spoken chunk shortened, selection made into a gesture.

> *"Ship the smallest complete correct thing."* and *"Layers and abstractions earn their place on concrete triggers — a second caller, a second implementation — never on a diagram."*

Two sequencing rules. **#18 before any other memory work** — the tests are the enabler and `select_memories` currently ships with none. **#24 after the mic-constraints decision**, because it tunes one half of a calibrated pair whose other half is under active redesign.

## Tier 3 — research bets

Nothing here should be built on the strength of this document.

- **The personalization session** (`pm-request/personalization-session-2026-08-14.md`, dated the same day, status "nothing built, nothing green-lit"). It already contains this exercise's best answers: beat-gapped listening that excludes the room's own voice **by time rather than by filtering** — which makes AEC unnecessary and lets `echoCancellation` go off, killing the system-wide ducking bug at the same time — and one structured call per beat returning `{say, draw, expect}`, so speech and drawing ride two lanes that never touch the model. Any spoken presence work belongs there, not in `voice.ts`.
- **`last_included_at` on memories**, renamed from "used" (the room can only observe what it injected, never what the model read), and sequenced after #18's `&[Memory]` refactor.
- **Draw-on reveal for freehand strokes only** — `pathLength={100}` + `stroke-dashoffset` on the `default` case (`SketchView.tsx:968`), where `strokePath` emits a single subpath. Rect and ellipse are built by two-pass loops emitting 8 and 2 subpaths, so they either complete at 50% of the duration and then retrace, or dash in lockstep and do not draw at all. Weigh against the fact that a newly-created sketch stages nothing.
- **The room's own clock on Home** — worth wanting, but only via a new single `next_scheduled_run` query with its own decided degradation. `nextRunAt` is on `Schedule`, not `Workflow`; the existing path is one IPC call per workflow.
- **Per-file graph edges in the viewer header** — only via a *narrow* per-file edge query. `room_graph` is a full-room O(n²) rebuild under the room mutex the chat turn holds.

## Where the findings compound — read this twice

These are the highest-value output of the exercise, and they are invisible in any single dimension's report.

**A. The motionless window is closed by three tiny fixes, none of them an animation.** Latency, motion and prior-art each independently proposed decorating the send→first-token wait; adversarial review found the pulsing `.agent-dot` already covers the model load and the entire prompt-eval, because `run_agent` emits `plan` before any model call. The genuinely dead stretch is **send → plan**, and its contents are the cold daemon (#2), the mislabelled embed window (#12) and the unstyled busy hook (#6). Fix those three and the window mostly disappears. A nib would have decorated a window that was mismeasured.

**B. One widened `refreshPrivacy` closes four presence findings and deletes an IPC call.** Move #13 fixes Home's wrong words while scanning, Home's frozen count, the dead CTA, and supplies the `scanning` boolean that move #14 needs — while removing a duplicate `privacyStatus()` call. Presence proposed four separate mechanisms for these; one query, correctly placed, is smaller than any of them.

**C. Voice's silent drops and hands-free's non-gesture send are the same bug.** Voice found the two bare `continue`s. Independently, the same review traced that hands-free turn 2+ invokes `send()` from a dictation completion callback — not a gesture — so WKWebView will never resume a suspended AudioContext. The mode designed for a user who is not looking at the screen is the mode structurally guaranteed to go mute *silently*. Move #3 is the precondition for anyone ever diagnosing this, and therefore the precondition for all spoken-presence work.

**D. Two proposals cancel each other — ship the deletion, not both.** Embodiment proposed restoring the "file open" line via a `data-open-file` attribute *and* passing `viewing` on `ask`. They answer to **different doors**: `ui_tools_specs` is structurally LocalEngine-only and router-gated (`agent.rs:2415-2418`), while `viewing` rides the user message through the ordinary privacy seam to every engine on every turn. Publishing the same fact twice through two mechanisms, only one of which reaches the case that motivated it, is worse than either. Delete the dead selector (#7); build `viewing` (#17).

**E. The memory dimension fails three times in one direction.** Bytes counted as chars, a silent truncation of the user's own typed Hebrew, and no event when the agent writes — all "the room knows and does not say", all under a function with zero tests. #18 and #9 are one story; write the tests once and all three fixes ship proven.

**F. Chat ordering delivers most of what the handoff-recap idea promised, for a tenth of the cost.** Initiative wanted the room to hand back a loose end on unlock; that was refuted (wrong sentence, wrong voice, material that barely exists, two pieces of persisted state that do not exist anywhere in the tree). Ordering by last activity (#19) makes the thread you were actually in the thread you return to — which is the same felt continuity, with no new state, no new writes, and no model call on the unlock path.

**G. The one-model-lane constraint was found independently by four dimensions.** Presence counted five existing consumers (privacy scanner, quiet filler, embedding backfill, `front_page_suggestions` on every unlock, `generate_ui_text` on Home). Initiative added a sixth: `memory_suggestion` fires a structured call after **every** non-empty assistant reply. Latency found the warm/idle disagreement. Prior art found the design doc that says it plainly: *"on a local room there is exactly one model lane. Any design that says 'five agents talk while it draws' is describing a cloud room, or lying."* Consequence: **no move in this document adds a seventh consumer**, and any future one must say which existing consumer it displaces. Note also that two existing loops facing this contention chose *opposite* policies — the scanner **pauses and resumes**, the filler **abandons the pass** — with nothing documenting why. That inconsistency decides whether the room's background work comes back after you speak to it, which is exactly the difference between something alive and something merely busy.

**H. "Do not add ambient motion" is the strongest agreement in the exercise, and it is a negative.** Motion found the reverted marching-ants edge with its written reason. Prior art found `composer.css:788-791` — *"this system allows no constant movement"* — with the history of deleting a 1.4 s pulse. Presence proposed pulsing two static dots and did not notice that `recording.css:258-263` states the scarcity out loud: *"The one continuous animation in the product, and it is the message: a capture is running."* Three dimensions converged; only one of them noticed it was converging on a prohibition.

## What must not be built

> *"Names should tell the truth"* governs this entire section. A liveness cue that does not correspond to work actually happening is a fabrication, and this app refuses fabrication everywhere else.

1. **A nib or writing cursor during the thinking state.** `.stream-cursor` is a *writing* cursor and `chat.css:882-885` says so in its own words. Rendering it while `streamText` is empty asserts text is being written when no token has been produced. Use `.composer-card.busy`, which claims only *busy*.
2. **A fourth "Thinking" step chip.** `.chat-route`, the agent strip with its running dot, and the two existing chips already say it. The proposal's own risk field — "gate it on the FIRST round only, or the strip becomes a wall" — is the tell. > *"YAGNI is design discipline. Imagined requirements deserve no real complexity."*
3. **A presence drone or room tone under the wait.** Continuous ambient sound, in an app with no volume control, gated only by a *motion* query, corresponding to nothing more specific than "a turn is open" — and its own wall-clock cap re-creates the ambiguity it was built to remove (silence after the drone would mean both "still working" and "gave up"). Its justification, "nothing happens during the wait", is false: AgentGraph and live step-chips render in that same bubble from real events.
4. **A typed "Hmm, let me see…" filler.** No result exists for fillers in a typed interface. CUI '25's non-spoken condition — a processing icon plus a sound effect — is precisely the one that showed **no significant effect**, while natural spoken fillers did. It would also pad the transcript saved to the room.
5. **Any spoken acknowledgement routed through `speakText`.** `speakText` calls `cancelAll()`, which sets `streamedTurn = false`, which permanently disables `feedStreamDelta` — **the answer would never be spoken** — and it fires `maybeFireTurnDone` → `onTurnAudioDone`, re-arming the mic mid-generation. > *"Every new I/O path gets a decided failure behavior: a secondary feature never breaks the primary path."*
6. **Any injected static delay to simulate deliberation.** CHI '26 names "simple, static delays—technical signals to simulate 'thinking'" as the failure mode its context-aware pacing beats; CUI '25 measured artificial wait chrome as having no significant effect. And the load-bearing result for a typed room: at N=240, responses at 2 s were rated **less** thoughtful than at 9–20 s, with trust unmoved. Honest deliberation time is not a liability here.
7. **Synthetic progress bars, percentages or ETAs over local work.** `ensure_up` polls a socket and genuinely does not know how far along it is. A bar that races then stalls destroys trust faster than no indicator. Move #1 exists because this rule is *already* being broken.
8. **Engagement-shaped aliveness** — farewell hooks, "I noticed you've been away", unprompted emotional address. HBS 2025 measured six such tactics in 37% of AI-companion farewells, boosting post-goodbye engagement up to **16×** driven by reactance-anger, while raising perceived manipulation, churn intent and negative word of mouth. One well-being-focused app showed none of it, so it is not inevitable. The app's own annotation comment already states the posture: an invented flourish "would be the UI manufacturing an opinion and attributing it to nobody."
9. **Pulsing the two persistent busy dots.** It overrides a written product decision about scarcity for a cosmetic gain. > *"One obvious way per codebase — write the house's idiom, not your own"* — which first requires reading what the house wrote down about why it is scarce.
10. **Word-synced highlighting gated on the chat-privacy receipt.** `askPrivacy` describes the **chat-model** door; the speech seam runs its own switch-blind redactor (`privacy.rs:314-320`, `speech_cmds.rs:45-55`), so the proposed gate opens the highlight exactly on the turns where the offsets are guaranteed not to match the screen. Also, `demon` plays at 0.88× and `ancient` plays three sources at once — there is no single word-to-time mapping. > *"Strangers should not be reached through chains of strangers."*
11. **Proactive unprompted action mid-task.** Measured cost of getting the timing wrong: 3–27% more time, 2× errors, 31–106% more annoyance versus the same delivery at a boundary. On one lane a proactive thought also queues the user's next question behind it. If it ever ships, it ships as a **question at a boundary**, never an action mid-task.
12. **A general "ambient work" event channel.** After the cheap moves, exactly one silent producer remains, and one producer is not a channel. Its silence is also already an explicit decision with a written reason (`backfill.rs:3-5, 194-198`: keyword retrieval stays fully functional), so the proposed back-off emit should be dropped too. > *"Errors should never pass silently. Unless explicitly silenced."*
13. **Per-file `room_graph` calls.** A full-room O(n²) rebuild under the room mutex the chat turn holds, re-fired on every file change.

## Through-line constraints

Every move above respects all six. Any future move must state how it does.

- **One model lane locally.** `worker_parallel = 1` for local engines; one running job slot, FIFO, `MAX_QUEUED = 10`. Six consumers already exist. A new model call is not free time — it is time taken from the answer the user is waiting for. Say which consumer yields.
- **The consent door.** `[data-agent-blocked]` enforced twice in code (walker skip + act-time re-check), no `set_setting` tool, agent workflows saved as DRAFTS, `due_schedules` firing only enabled schedules of active workflows. The room cannot switch itself on. Nothing here relaxes that line; a room that could schedule itself would read as unsupervised, not alive.
- **The privacy seams are plural and independent.** The chat-model door and the speech redactor are different doors with similar words — never gate one on the other's receipt. `speakable_text` refuses outright when web access is off, so the entire spoken lane is opt-in. Filenames already ride in the system inventory; *content* does not, and the paperclip is the user's gesture, not ours to pre-spend.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`.** CSS coverage is effectively complete and every keyframe lands on its resting value when switched off; JS has one canonical helper. A cue whose only job is to be *noticed* keeps a static form (the ghost ring) rather than disappearing. Reduced motion must never mean reduced information — which is the whole defect in move #23.
- **The byte-stable system prompt (ADD-22).** Per-turn facts go in the always-new user message. `custom_instructions` is the uncapped standing-instruction channel and already lives in the prefix — that, not a memory row, is where "answer in Hebrew" belongs.
- **House conventions are the contract.** Pure Rust logic gets `#[cfg(test)] mod tests` in the same file; pure TS gets a page-script test that slices the source and transpiles it (no imports from `src/**`, no exports added for testability); a one-attribute contract gets a two-file textual assertion. Red-first is not optional — a test written alongside a constant change that cannot fail on the unchanged constant is ceremony.

**One practicality kept against purity, recorded here:** move #20 duplicates a single piece of state (`openingFileId`) rather than making `FileContent` optional across every consumer of the open-file shape. Duplication is cheaper than reshaping a type the whole pane reads, for one call site.
